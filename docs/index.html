What Are Aircraft?

Aircraft are machines capable of flight. From small training aircraft to advanced fighter jets, all rely on Lift, Weight, Thrust and Drag.

The Four Forces Of Flight

Lift acts upward. Weight pulls downward. Thrust pushes the aircraft forward. Drag slows it down.
