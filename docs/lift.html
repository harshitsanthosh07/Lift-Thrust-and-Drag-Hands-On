What is lift?

Lift is a force that opposes weight, and planes use this as an advantage to fly in the air. This can be explained by Newton's 3rd law. Fun fact: Birds need lift to fly!

What happens if lift never existed?

Planes would not have been able to fly and would have stayed a concept instead of reality. Also birds wouldn't have been able to fly :]

How is lift created?

Lift is created when air flows around the wing of a plane, which is specifically shaped to create an area of low pressure above the wing and higher pressure underneath. This pressure creates lift.
