What is drag?

Drag is the force that opposes motion. This can cause the airplane to decelerate. Drag changes according to speed and shape of moving object.

How can drag be reduced?

We would have to make the shape of the object in motion more aerodynamic. This would help air flow more smoothly past the accelerating object by reducing places where air could catch onto the plane.

What happens if there is no drag?

You might think that if drag didn't exist, objects can move faster and there would be less force required to accelerate. But drag is important, as without it objects will keep moving unless opposite force is applied. Parachutes will also be useless if drag was non-existant.
